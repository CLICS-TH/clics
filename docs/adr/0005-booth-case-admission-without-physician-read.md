# Booth cases are admitted by AI safety review and the physician sandbox, not a physician read

A booth case enters the case pool only after a separate AI clinical-safety review of every question variant and option, against current Thai and WHO guidance, and after the physician sandbox shows its difficulty matches the pool (every mean within ±10 of the pool median, best path at least 15 above the weakest). No physician reads the cases before the NFCME-2026 booth: the owner has no physician crew available before 3 Nov 2026, and the case pool grew from one case to eleven. This supersedes #215's acceptance criterion that a physician reads the case. The admission marker lives in the case file, the loader leaves out any case without it, and a superadmin can switch a single case off during the forum.

## Consequences

- A clinical error that both the AI review and the "no withhold or delay" test miss reaches a national CME audience; the per-case off switch is the only remedy at the booth.
- The sandbox measures difficulty, not clinical truth.
