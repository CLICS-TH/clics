## Difficulty re-check (ADR 0005)

Each entry is judged as the mean of 3 plays against the pool of this run's cases and the committed tables of 1 more, each on its current tree: every entry within its band of the pool median, and best at least 6.59 above weakest. A fitted entry draws on all 81 of the case's samples, so it is steadier than a 3-play mean. One entry's noise is about 0.21σ², not σ²/3, so the bands are a little lenient. Best − weakest's is about 0.58σ², not 2σ²/3, so the gap is a little strict. Here σ² is one play's variance.
